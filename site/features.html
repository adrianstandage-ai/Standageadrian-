Features

Everything a small landlord actually needs. Nothing they don't.

PropNet isn't trying to be Xero or QuickBooks. It's the simple bit in between a notebook and an accountant.

Know what you're actually making

Open the app and your net profit for the tax year is the first thing you see, with money in, money out and how this month compares to last.

  • Filter long-term lets and holiday lets separately
  • Per-property income, expenses and history
  • Works across all your properties at once

Log it in seconds, not at year end

Income or expense, pick the property, type the amount. Attach a photo of the receipt or invoice if you have one.

  • Receipt photos stored with the entry
  • Split one bill across several properties
  • Repeat monthly, quarterly or yearly so rent logs itself
  • Mortgage interest and finance costs tracked separately

Your figures, grouped by SA105 box

Expenses are sorted into the boxes on the SA105 property pages, with a Section 24 finance-cost estimate, so you or your accountant can check them quickly.

  • SA105-style summary on screen
  • Export as PDF and CSV for your accountant PRO
  • Clear notes on what PropNet assumes

Safety and deadline dates, in one list

Set reminders for the dates you can't afford to miss. You choose what applies to your property, because rules differ across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

  • Gas safety, EICR, insurance, deposit protection and more
  • Self Assessment deadlines
  • Add to your phone's calendar in one tap

See the year at a glance

Income against expenses month by month, and a rough tax pot so January's bill is less of a surprise. It's a rough guide, not a calculation of what you owe.

  • Monthly income vs expenses chart
  • Rough tax estimate after the Section 24 credit
  • Search and filter every entry

Who it's for

Made for people who rent out a few properties themselves.

Self-managing landlords

One to five properties, maybe with a job on the side. You want your records sorted without paying for accounting software you'll never fully use.

Airbnb and holiday-let hosts

Bookings, platform fees, cleaning and laundry all logged per property, with holiday lets shown separately from long-term lets.

Probably not for you if…

You run a large portfolio through a limited company, need full bookkeeping, or need software to file Making Tax Digital returns. PropNet keeps records only.
